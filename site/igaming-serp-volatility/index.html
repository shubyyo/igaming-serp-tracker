      How it works

      1. 1
        Fetch. 50 frozen keywords per market, Google mobile top 10, every day.
      2. 2
        Guard. A result that doesn't look like its own last week is re-fetched; agreeing oddities wait a day for confirmation.
      3. 3
        Score. Rank changes weighted toward the top, trimmed mean per market, scaled against that market's 90-day history.
